# TRACE-X

### From Fragmented Signals to Verified Investigative Leads

TRACE-X is an intelligence-fusion platform that connects entities, aliases, relationships, and signals from multiple sources to help discover, analyze, and prioritize investigative leads.

---

## Overview

Investigative information is often spread across different sources. TRACE-X brings these signals together and helps identify meaningful connections between entities and activities.

The platform provides a unified view of intelligence and helps users understand relationships, detect unusual patterns, prioritize leads, and trace findings back to supporting evidence.

---

## Features

- **Entity Resolution**  
  Connect aliases, identifiers, and related entities across different sources.

- **Relationship Mapping**  
  Discover connections between entities and visualize relationships.

- **Network Discovery**  
  Identify hidden relationships, clusters, and connected entities.

- **Threat Detection**  
  Detect unusual activity, anomalies, and emerging patterns.

- **Lead Prioritization**  
  Rank investigative leads based on their relevance and available signals.

- **Evidence Traceability**  
  Understand why a lead was identified and trace it back to supporting evidence.

- **Investigation Dashboard**  
  View important entities, relationships, signals, and findings from one place.

- **Reporting**  
  Convert validated findings into structured intelligence reports.

Investigation Flow

TRACE-X follows a simple workflow:

Collect relevant signals from available sources.
Connect related entities and identifiers.
Discover relationships and patterns.
Analyze unusual or significant activity.
Prioritize the most relevant leads.
Validate findings using supporting evidence.
Report validated intelligence.
